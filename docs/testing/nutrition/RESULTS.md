# Nutrition description update

Source: USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy April 2018, https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/ . Values are per 100 g; calculation sums ingredient grams × reference nutrient / 100. Numeric recipe ranges use their midpoint. ML conversions use the densities recorded in ingredient-reference.json. Whole milk 3.25% fat and heavy cream are generic proxies; actual brands can differ. Egg portions assume 50 g edible egg. Water includes plain soda water and ice. Coffee grounds are discarded and excluded; espresso is counted. Basil/chilli oils use olive oil as a proxy. No health claims are made. These are estimates, not certified nutrition labels.

No incomplete recipe receives an invented total. Unknown premixes, unspecified portions, nutrient omissions and named garnishes without complete amounts block the estimate. Each outlet item displays all four requested nutrient labels or an explicit unavailable notice. Details, assumptions and missing inputs are in recipe-calculations.json. Customisations are excluded. Prices, availability, images and order history are untouched.
